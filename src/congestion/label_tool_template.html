Congestion labels

Practice 0/0 Test 0/0

Judge only the road inside the orange outline. When the outlines cover two roads or directions that differ, label the more congested one.

Free-flowing: vehicles could move freely. Moderate: busy, gaps of about a car length or less, but no queue. Congested: a queue of vehicles nose to tail over much of the outline.

At night, judge from the headlights and tail lights. Skip only when the road inside the outline cannot be seen (heavy rain, fog, glare); a replacement from the same camera and time band appears.

The date and time are hidden on purpose, so that the label comes from the picture alone.
