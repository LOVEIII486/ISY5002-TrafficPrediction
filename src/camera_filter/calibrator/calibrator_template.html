How to use

  1. The pink points are two weeks of detector output; each point is the bottom-centre of one box. Fixed false positives form a tight cluster in one place.
  2. Each camera opens with a suggested draft (road region + detection zone). Drag the vertices to adjust, then click "Confirm". Tab dot: orange = draft to confirm, green = confirmed.
  3. Road region: include every road vehicles use (both directions, ramps); err on the wide side. Every box outside it is removed.
  4. Exclusion set: after the road region, box only the fixed false positives that remain on the road (road markings, gantries). Can be limited to day or night.
  5. Detection zone: a near section of one direction, covering all its lanes, long enough for 5–15 queuing vehicles. Used for congestion analysis.
  6. When all 8 cameras are done, click "Export config" to save the JSON file.

Scroll to zoom; right-drag or hold Space to pan. Ctrl+Z to undo, Delete to remove the selected shape.
