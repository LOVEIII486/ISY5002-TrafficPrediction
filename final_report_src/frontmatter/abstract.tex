\section*{Abstract}

Traffic monitoring systems observe road conditions, while short-term forecasting
anticipates congestion. This project develops a modular prototype that treats
the two tasks separately because the available data support different levels of
measurement. A vehicle-recognition module fine-tunes a COCO-pretrained YOLO26m
on MIO-TCD and on 320 Singapore LTA camera images with teacher pseudo-labels,
and is evaluated on a leakage-aware MIO-TCD split and on LTA teacher agreement.
A forecasting module compares eight models on PEMSD8 under native and unified
nine-channel inputs, with five-seed paired checks for the leading four; the
custom MultiHeadSTGCN is examined through a staged ablation. A web workbench
presents camera monitoring, vehicle counts, forecast curves and a
threshold-based risk ranking, while labeling the data source of each view. On
MIO-TCD, fine-tuning raises mAP@0.5 from 0.430 to 0.720. On PEMSD8, GWNET
achieves the lowest MAE among the evaluated configurations, and the custom model
is competitive but not superior. The prototype does not close the loop from LTA
images to local forecasting; doing so requires calibrated, time-aligned traffic
series at the camera locations.
